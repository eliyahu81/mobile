Privacy Policy – Nova PDF Studio

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Nova PDF Studio respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use it.

PDF Files and Documents

PDF documents opened, viewed, created, or edited in Nova PDF Studio remain on the user's device unless the user explicitly chooses to use a feature that requires transferring or sharing a file.

Nova PDF Studio does not sell users' PDF documents or personal files.

Permissions

The application may request access to files, storage, camera, or other device features when these permissions are necessary for functionality selected by the user.

Payments

Purchases offered through the Android application are processed using Google Play's billing system. Nova PDF Studio does not directly receive or store users' complete payment-card information.

Third-Party Services

Some application features may rely on third-party services. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

Nova PDF Studio is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, its functionality, or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Nova PDF Studio Google Play listing.
